Two independent fixes

Shared parser: day/month interpreted month/day

Day-first tenants using the shared parser get swapped month and day. Example: R-41200, Harbour Dental, app: 7 April becomes 4 July. This group also includes app/email reports from booking-v3 tenants; the tenant’s form alone does not identify the cause. See parser ticket.

Make parsing respect the tenant’s date convention or accept an unambiguous date representation. Test ambiguous dates in both directions, impossible dates, app and email paths, day-first locales and a month-first control. This is a proposed fix, not an implemented one.

booking-v3: wrong date-format hint

For a day-first tenant, booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY while the form interprets input in the tenant’s order. A person following that hint can therefore enter the wrong date even after the shared parser is fixed. See form-hint ticket.

Generate the hint and input parsing convention from the same tenant locale. Test Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour through the form, plus booking-v2 and app/email controls. Confirm what the user sees and the date actually saved.

No unsupported third cause

The five reports listed in desk-notes.json describe one-day moves. They remain in the parser group under the exported tenant-based logic and must not be counted twice. Their wording deserves investigation but does not establish a timezone or daylight-saving root cause. Keep uncertainty explicit; create another cause only after reproducing it.

Received is not merged

A spike in merging is not a spike in incoming failures. Track receivedAt and mergedAt separately. The product serves about 300k people, but this desk covers multiple products and the export has no affected-user denominator: do not infer a failure rate or put every report under sched-prod without evidence.

Replacement triage rule

  1. Deduplicate export rows by report id before counting. Equal ids are one report; matching subjects are not enough.
  2. Read the body and check tenant locale, tenant form, and channel. Use causeOf and sameCause; intakeTag and browserLocale are clues only.
  3. Merge only when the same verified cause applies. Keep independent causes in separate tickets even with identical subjects.
  4. Unknown tenant or unreproduced cause: retain for investigation, do not merge automatically.
  5. Keep report ids, counts, evidence and issue links auditable. Review new reports and re-render before relying on this snapshot.

Two independent problems

Parser: day/month text is interpreted month/day

Issue #1 covers the shared parser path for day-first tenants. R-41200 says 7 April became 4 July. This includes app and email reports even when their tenant uses booking-v3, and booking-v2 form reports. The original intake tag does not decide membership.

Proposed remedy: parse according to the tenant convention or use an unambiguous representation. Verify ambiguous date pairs in both directions, invalid dates, affected app/email and booking-v2 paths, and a month-first control. No product fix has been implemented.

The five reports listed in desk-notes.json are counted here and retained as caveats: R-41202 and R-41620 describe a one-day change; R-41209 mentions June; R-41344 gives different days in Lisbon and Toronto; R-41236 says an invite arrived before booking. The supplied desk view footnotes them under parser. Their wording alone does not establish a timezone, daylight-saving or delivery-order cause. Reproduce them before claiming this parser fix covers their symptoms.

Hint: booking-v3 gives the wrong format instruction

Issue #2 covers the form channel for day-first tenants using booking-v3. It prints MM/DD/YYYY while the form reads input in the tenant convention. Following the instruction can therefore produce a swapped date. This is not fixed by correcting the shared parser alone.

Generate the hint and input convention from the same locale. Test the form at Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour, verifying the date saved as well as the displayed instruction. Use booking-v2 and app/email as controls. Do not assign all reports from a booking-v3 tenant to this cause: the channel matters.
